# How to write a framework

The first framework, about writing frameworks. Delete it once you have three of your own.

## Why this folder matters most

Reference material stops Claude being **wrong**. Frameworks stop it being **generic**. The model
already knows the textbook version of your job. What it cannot know is the order you do things in,
the judgment calls you make, and where you draw the line on quality.

## The test

Hand the note to a new joiner. If they would produce work you would sign off, it is a framework.
If they would still have to ask you how, it is just reference material. Rewrite it.

## Shape of a good one

1. **When to use this.** One line, so the agent knows whether to reach for it.
2. **The steps, in your order.** Numbered. Include the boring ones; those are the ones people skip.
3. **The judgment calls.** Where you decide something, say what you weigh and which way you lean.
4. **What good looks like.** Concrete and checkable.
5. **What bad looks like.** Often more useful, because it draws the line.
6. **Gotchas.** The things you learned the hard way.

## Worth writing first

Pick the task you have explained to someone else more than twice. That is your highest-value
framework, every time.
