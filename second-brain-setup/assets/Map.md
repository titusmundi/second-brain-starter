# Map

The index. What lives where, and when to reach for it. Claude reads this first, so keep it
current: if you add a folder or a major note, add a line here.

## The two spaces

| Space | Who it is for | What goes in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Thinking/` | **you** | One idea per note, in your own words, linked. Processing, not storage. |
| `Doing/` | **the agent** | The hierarchy below. Context an agent needs to do real work. |

They are separate on purpose. AI output never lands in your thinking space, and project tracking
never sits next to the work that has no deadline.

## Doing

| Folder | Reach for it when | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[About Me]] | Always. Loaded first. | Role, preferences, standards, goals. |
| `01 - Frameworks/` | A recurring task needs doing *your* way | SOPs, how you work, what good and bad mean. |
| `02 - Examples/` | Output needs to match your taste | `good/` and `bad/` exemplars. |
| `03 - Knowledge Base/` | A fact about your world is needed | Past work, reference material, org context. |
| `03 - Knowledge Base/People/` | A person comes up | One short note each: role, owns, needs from me, waiting on. |
| `04 - Projects/` | Anything about live work | Active state, phases, dated decisions. |

## Start here
- [[About Me]] is the highest-value file in this vault. Fill it in first.
- [[Projects Index]] lists live work.
- [[How to write a framework]] explains the folder that does the most work.
