# About Me

> [!important] This is the highest-value file in the vault, because it is the part no model can
> guess. Fill it in badly and fix it later; do not leave it empty.
>
> **Fastest way to fill it:** launch Claude Code at this folder and say
> *"Read Doing/Map.md and About Me.md, then ask me the three questions you most need answered to
> be useful to me."* Then answer in plain English and let it write the file.

## Role and scope
_What you own, and what you do not. Being clear about the edges matters as much as the middle._

## Who I work with, and what they need from me
_Name them and say what each one actually wants. "Wants trend, not detail" is useful. "Manager"
is not._

## What I produce most weeks
_The recurring outputs. This is what Claude will end up helping with most._

## What I am working toward this quarter
_The goal, so suggestions can be pointed at it._

### Preferences
_How you want to be worked with. Be specific and slightly fussy; this is the section that saves
you the most repetition._
- Example: explain in small steps and confirm between them, rather than one large batch.
- Example: stop and confirm before anything irreversible or outward-facing.
- Example: verify before claiming something is done.

### Standards (what "good" means to me)
_Fill this in with real sentences. "Clear" is not useful. "Leads with the decision, then the
reasoning, and fits on one screen" is useful, because it can be checked._
