# Good and bad, side by side

How to use this folder, plus one worked example. Replace the example with your own work.

## Why bad examples are worth more

A good example shows the target. A **bad** one shows the line, and the line is the thing an agent
cannot infer. "Make it clearer" means nothing. Two versions with "this one, not that one" means
everything.

Keep both in this folder. Real output, lightly anonymised if it needs to be.

## Worked example: a status update

### Bad
> We have been making good progress on the reporting work this sprint. There were a few
> challenges with the data but the team has been working hard and we are hopeful that things
> will be in a better place soon. Happy to discuss further.

Why it is bad: no number, no date, no decision. "Hopeful" is not a status. The reader still has to
ask the question they came with.

### Good
> Reporting is 6 of 9 views live. The remaining 3 are blocked on the Jira field being populated,
> owned by the platform team, expected 12 Aug. No change to the overall date.

Why it is good: leads with the number, names the blocker and its owner, states the date impact.
Fits in a glance.

## The pattern
Lead with the answer. Name the number. Say who owns the blocker. State the date impact, including
when it is "no change".
